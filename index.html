How it works

Three small steps from box to glow.

1

Open the back

Turn the house around. The opening at the bottom of the back wall is made for a tealight.

2

Slide in a light

Place a tealight or small LED candle inside and set the house on a windowsill or shelf.

3

Watch it glow

The light shines through the windowpanes, so the whole house looks lived in after dark.

Inspired by the canals

The narrow, tall houses along Amsterdam’s canals each have their own face. Huislichtje turns that look into a small light for your home, printed in one piece with every window, door and step.
